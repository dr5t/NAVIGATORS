1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing and using the Navigators Inertial Dead Reckoning (IDR) Simulator, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions. This application is a prototype built for demonstration purposes.

2. Device Sensor Permissions

To function correctly, this application requires explicit access to your device's hardware sensors. We request and utilize the following permissions:

  • Geolocation API: Used strictly to initialize your starting position and to provide absolute ground truth updates when a high-accuracy signal is available.
  • DeviceMotion (Accelerometer): Used to measure linear acceleration. This data is fed into our local AI model to estimate your walking speed and velocity when GPS is denied.
  • DeviceOrientation (Gyroscope & Compass): Used to determine your absolute heading (direction) and rotation rates to map your trajectory over time.

3. Data Processing & Privacy

Your privacy is protected by our architecture. Unlike traditional navigation apps, Navigators IDR is an Edge-AI application. This means:

  • All AI inference (speed estimation) and sensor fusion (Extended Kalman Filter) happens locally in your browser.
  • Your real-time location and motion data is never uploaded to external servers during normal use.
  • The only time data is transmitted is if you explicitly choose to "Record Trip Data", which sends the recorded sensor logs to your local PC for model training.

4. Limitation of Liability

This software is a prototype demonstrating infrastructure-independent navigation. The estimated positions provided during GNSS outages are mathematical approximations. Do not rely on this application for critical navigation, safety of life, or emergency routing.
