1. Software Operating Scope

Navigators provides infrastructure-independent inertial dead reckoning positioning software designed to maintain continuous position estimation during GNSS signal outages. By accessing or utilizing the software, you agree to comply with these Terms and Conditions.

2. Hardware Permissions & Sensor Grant

Operation requires granting client browser permissions for location (Geolocation API) and inertial motion hardware (DeviceMotion / DeviceOrientation). All telemetry data processed by the application is evaluated locally on-device.

3. Safety Disclaimer & Limitation of Liability

Dead reckoning calculations during GNSS signal denial represent mathematical and neural network estimates. Navigators is not a certified life-safety or sole-source navigation instrument. Users assume full responsibility for vehicle and pedestrian safety.
