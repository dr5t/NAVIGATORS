Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that allow web applications to remember your state and preferences across sessions. In modern web apps, this also includes technologies like localStorage and Cache Storage.

2. How We Use Local Storage

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics tracking (like Google Analytics). All local storage usage is strictly functional to ensure the app works offline.

  • Map Data Cache: When you click "Save area offline", the OpenStreetMap road geometry for your area is saved to your browser's Cache Storage.
  • AI Model Cache: Our neural network model (`model.onnx`) is cached locally so the app loads instantly without the internet.
  • Consent Preferences: If you accept or reject cookies via our popup, we save that preference in `localStorage` so we don't ask you again.

3. Managing Your Data

You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data/cache. Doing so will remove your downloaded offline maps and require you to redownload them when you regain internet access.
