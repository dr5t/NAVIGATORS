DEMO FIELD GUIDE

Three moments.
One continuous position.

  1. Start with a fix.

    Choose Walking and allow location, motion, and compass access. Hold the phone screen-up with its top forward. GPS initializes your position; steps and compass estimate movement during loss. Vehicle mode requires calibration and its AI buffer.

  2. Take the signal away.

    Choose Online streets, then Save area offline (a 2 km square around the map center). Wait for offline files to be ready, reload once, disconnect internet, and select Simulate GNSS outage. Navigation continues locally.

  3. Bring GPS back.

    Select Restore GPS. Reacquisition brings the estimate back gradually. Export device timings after the run.

Android installation: open the secure app URL in Chrome, choose the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen → Install. Keep the app open while recording.

Use saved playback for an illustrative trajectory. Real accuracy claims require a recorded, evaluated trip.
