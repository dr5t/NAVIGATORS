1. On-Device Edge Architecture

Navigators is engineered around an on-device edge architecture. Inertial sensor fusion (6-DOF IMU), neural network speed estimation (ONNX WebAssembly), and geometric map matching execute locally inside your client web browser. Your live geographic coordinates, accelerometer readings, and gyroscope telemetry are not transmitted to external cloud servers during active navigation sessions.

2. Hardware Sensor Access & Usage

The application requests access to hardware sensors strictly to calculate location and relative trajectory updates:

  • Geolocation API: Used to acquire initial GNSS position fixes and calibrate absolute coordinates.
  • Accelerometer (DeviceMotion): Used for velocity estimation and step tracking during GNSS outages.
  • Gyroscope & Magnetometer (DeviceOrientation): Used to measure rotational rate and vehicle heading.

3. Local Storage & Data Retention

Navigators uses local browser storage technologies (CacheStorage, IndexedDB, and localStorage) to store functional navigation assets, including offline OpenStreetMap street tiles and local bookmarks. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are deployed.

4. Third-Party Map Tile Requests

When operating in Online Map mode, map tile image requests are fetched directly from OpenStreetMap servers (`tile.openstreetmap.org`). When operating in Offline Map mode, street geometry data is retrieved exclusively from locally cached vector files.
