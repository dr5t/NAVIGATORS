# Navigators Product Rules

> **Mandatory rules for every current and future feature in the Navigators product line.**

---

## 1. Absolutely Prohibited

The following practices, UI elements, and marketing patterns are strictly forbidden:

### Visual & UI Constraints
- No popup gradients
- No vague hero text
- No excessive scroll animation
- No unnecessary animation
- No excessive pill-shaped buttons
- No emoji icons
- No cursor animation
- No glassmorphism-heavy UI
- No neon / cyberpunk styling
- No excessive glowing effects
- No excessive floating cards

### Authenticity & Data Integrity
- No fake counters
- No fake reviews
- No fake testimonials
- No fake metrics
- No fake accuracy claims
- No fake user numbers
- No fake contribution numbers
- No fake navigation statistics
- No fake AI status
- No fake GNSS status
- No fake offline status
- No unsupported product claims
- No unfinished feature presented as functional

### AI & Copywriting Constraints
- No giant AI marketing sections
- No AI-generated images
- No fabricated photographs
- No "Made with AI" tag
- No generic AI marketing copy
- No AI-looking decorative copy

---

## 2. Required Standards

The following assets, pages, metadata, and structural requirements must be present and maintained:

### Metadata & Branding
- Site favicon
- Proper metadata
- Proper page titles
- Open Graph metadata
- Web manifest
- One official custom domain

### Legal & Informational Pages
- Privacy Policy page
- Terms & Conditions page
- Cookie Policy page
- About page
- Contact / Support page
- Custom 404 page

### Footer Specifications
- Professional footer
- Footer text: `Developed by Navigators`

---

## 3. Copywriting Rules

- Do not use an em dash anywhere in newly created product copy.
